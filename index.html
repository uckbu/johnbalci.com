Research

Embedded Software · Purdue CubeSat Lab

Electric Propulsion and Plasma Lab · August 2025–Present

I develop embedded C++ firmware for STM32 to turn raw capacitance measurements into real-time volumetric propellant telemetry. The system uses a custom parallel-plate capacitor setup with oversampling and averaging across 16–32 samples.

I also synthesized 1 V half-wave sinusoidal pulses in MATLAB, with a 20 µs duration and 1 Hz pulse repetition frequency, to characterize discharge cycles for a novel pulsed-magnetoplasmadynamic thruster in a 6U CubeSat.
